Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how mywellnesscalc.com ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website.

Information we collect

Calculator inputs: All calculations performed on this site (BMI, calories, water intake, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit the values you enter into our calculators. Your data stays on your device.

Analytics data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This may collect anonymised data such as pages visited, time on page, browser type, and approximate geographic location. This data cannot identify you personally.

Advertising: We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings, and you can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies primarily for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). These are third-party cookies and are governed by Google's privacy policy. By using our site, you consent to cookies as described here. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some functionality may be affected.

Affiliate disclosure

If a link is clicked and results in a purchase or signup, My Wellness Calc may earn a commission at no additional cost to the visitor. Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
