How the numbers are worked out

Named formulas

Calculators say which equation they use, such as Mifflin-St Jeor for calories, the U.S. Navy method for body fat and Epley for one-rep max, so you can look it up yourself.

Charts match the tools

Reference tables in our guides are recomputed with the same formula the calculator runs, so the chart and the tool give you the same answer.

Corrections are logged

When we get something wrong we fix it and record the change. Results are estimates, not medical advice.
