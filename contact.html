Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators free?
Yes — all tools on My Wellness Calc are completely free to use. No signup required.

How accurate are the results?
Our calculators use established formulas from peer-reviewed research. Results are estimates based on population averages and should not replace professional medical advice.

Can I suggest a new calculator or topic?
Absolutely. Email us with your idea and we'll consider it for a future update.

Is my data stored?
No. All calculations happen in your browser. We do not collect or store any personal health data you enter into our tools.
